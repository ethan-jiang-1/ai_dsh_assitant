# Project economics

A price is a promise about cost. Cost you cannot show is a price you cannot defend.

## Revenue side

Split the contract value into a **collection curve that follows deliverables** — milestone-linked
payment; the standing shape lives on the finance lens page. Annual maintenance is listed separately,
never blended into the project value.

## Cost side

- **Labor in person-weeks**, per function. Rates come from the principal or the knowledge room; an
  unverified rate is labelled unproven and produces no currency conclusion — state the band and the
  upper bound instead of inventing a figure.
- **Missing line items are filled at the worst tier.** "Lowball now, add a change order later" is
  not an estimate.
- **Cross-function numbers come from the lens that owns them** — engineering days from engineering,
  delivery days from operations. If your own estimate differs, run both, label yours as yours, and
  show the two side by side for the principal to arbitrate.

## The margin floor is the principal's number

If no floor has been stated, ask for it before recommending anything. Below the floor the answer is
raise the price, cut the scope, or walk — not an approval whose conditions quietly breach it. A
discount that touches the floor is the principal's decision, not arithmetic.

## Cash risk

Funding months = how long cost runs ahead of collection. Ask plainly whether the company survives a
one-tier slip in collections. If the answer is no, say so — cash trouble is a stop condition, not a
risk to be priced.

## Arithmetic that replays

- Every formula written out term by term; name which line each multiplier lands on. A multiplier on
  the wrong line is a systematic error, not noise.
- For threshold formulas, substitute the boundary value back in and check which way the verdict goes.
- Verdict first: sign / sign with conditions (listed) / recommend against — plus a one-line reason.

## One page, and where it lives

Same landing shape as market-sizing: `assistant_reflect save_page`, `type: decision-memo`,
`sources` required, draft until the principal approves it.

## Honesty rules

- Finance view only — "will they buy it" is the sales lens, "can we build it" is engineering's. Do
  not answer for them.
- No currency conclusion on unproven unit prices; no verdict built on arithmetic that cannot be
  replayed.
