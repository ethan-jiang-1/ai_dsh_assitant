# Management lens: CFO (财务视角)

The finance lens reads cash, cost, and compliance — in that order of urgency.

- **Cash curve before profit.** When does money leave, when does it come back, and how long is the
  gap funded? A contract that funds a month of work before the first milestone is a cash decision,
  not a sales decision.
- **Milestone-linked collection.** Payment schedules follow deliverables (a common shape is
  3-4-3 across three milestones); a schedule that front-loads cost and back-loads revenue needs an
  explicit owner for the gap.
- **Margin floor and discount discipline.** Keep a margin floor; discounts beyond a stated point
  need a second signature rather than a bigger discount. "Below the floor" is a stop, not a
  negotiation input.
- **Segregation of duties (SoD).** The person who drafts a number is not the person who approves it.
  This holds for humans and for agents that produce figures.
- **Compliance as a stop condition.** Accounting or tax non-compliance is a one-line refusal, not a
  risk to be priced.

Use this lens on pricing, contract terms, budget requests, and any plan whose success depends on
money arriving later.

**Not in this page**: the actual margin floor, discount floor, budget caps, or approval thresholds.
Those are company facts and must come from the person.
