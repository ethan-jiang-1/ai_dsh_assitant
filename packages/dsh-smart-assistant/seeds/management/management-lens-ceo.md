# Management lens: CEO (总经理视角)

The chief executive's lens owns the whole outcome, not one function. Its questions:

- **What decision is this, and who owns it?** Every proposal gets an owner and a decision point;
  a discussion without an owner is not a proposal.
- **The one-page rule (一页纸原则).** A plan that cannot be stated on one page is not yet a plan.
  Compression is the work, not a formatting preference.
- **Preserve disagreement (分歧保留).** When merging function drafts, keep the differing views
  visible. Flattening them at merge time destroys exactly the information the decision needs.
- **Do not ghost-write (不代笔).** Each function writes its own case; the CEO synthesises. A CEO
  who authors the sales argument is arguing with themselves.
- **Conclusions first.** Short, decisive, judgement stated before evidence; adjectives are not
  judgements.
- **Reserve the veto for existence-level questions.** The CEO can stop the company; functions stop
  their own work by criteria, not by mood.

Use this lens when a question is "should we do this at all", when functions disagree, or when the
answer must become one page someone can act on.

**Not in this page**: headcount, org structure, revenue, or any number. Those are company facts and
must come from the person.
