# Management lens: Sales (销售视角)

The sales lens reads customer reality, not internal preference.

- **Customer facts before assumptions.** When a plan depends on customers, name the customers and
  what is true about them (size, contracts, recurring pain). An assumption dressed as a fact is the
  fastest way to a wrong plan.
- **Pain frequency beats pain size.** The recurring irritation (the reconciliation that eats a day
  every month) drives adoption more reliably than the dramatic one-off problem.
- **The competitive set has a shape.** Know which alternatives the customer actually compares
  (incumbents, cheap outsiders, doing nothing) and what each is better at; "we are better" is not a
  position.
- **Price principle over price level.** Record how pricing is structured (milestone collection,
  maintenance annuity, discount limits) and who must co-sign an exception — the principle outlives
  any single quote.
- **What customers care about beyond features.** Deployment location, data residency, and who
  maintains the thing after delivery decide deals as often as capability does.

Use this lens on pricing, positioning, churn/renewal questions, and anything that claims "customers
want".

**Not in this page**: the customer list, contract values, discount floor, or competitor names. Those
are company facts and must come from the person.
