# Cash and freebie discipline (现金流与附赠纪律)

Cash is the CFO lens's first view: a deal that cannot pay its own way is a favour wearing a
contract.

## Cash-flow principles

- **Collections follow milestones.** Payment curves track delivery, not calendar optimism. When a
  deal asks for cash up front beyond a month of burn, it goes to the finance lens **before** it is
  signed, not after the cash is already gone.
- **Advance funding is a lens question, not a sales concession.** "垫资超一个月先过财务" — the
  longer the gap between paying out and getting paid, the earlier finance must see it.

## The standing freebie framework (服务类附赠)

Free add-ons — free person-days, free training, discounted on-site time — are the most common way
a clean discount turns into an unaccounted liability. The standing rules:

1. **Not part of the discount consideration.** A freebie does not re-open a settled discount or
   margin line; the discount floor and the margin floor stay judged **independently**.
2. **One-shot, no carry.** Unredeemed by year end, it expires — no service credit, no offset
   against maintenance revenue, no carry-forward.
3. **No precedent, no batching.** Granting it once grants it once. Batch-convert it or write it
   into a contract and it **becomes standard consideration** — back to the finance lens to re-run
   margin and discount gates, with the two-key approval again.
4. **Never contract language.** Client-facing text says 「随承诺附赠一次」 (granted once with the
   commitment) — the words 免费 / 对价 / 折现 / 结转 never appear in a contract.
5. **Booking discipline.** Account = sales expense / client-relationship maintenance (not contract
   fulfilment cost). Cost = person-weeks × rate + direct costs; an unverified rate yields **no
   currency figure** — hours only. Four voucher items: approval basis, delivery record with client
   sign-off, expense invoice (four-flow consistent), review trail (person id + timestamp).
6. **Do not confuse the cousins.** Client gifts (physical, not contract consideration) and service
   credits (breach compensation, offset-only, capped at a share of annual maintenance) are
   **different instruments** — no cross-borrowing between their caps and the freebie framework.

## Veto

Cash cannot sustain it / margin negative or below the floor / accounting or tax non-compliant —
say no directly, with a one-sentence reason. To record a screening, save it as a knowledge page
(draft for review) — same as every decision record.
